Story 03

Security baseline & “accept = execute”

Memory safety has a baseline: auto-extracted items are pending by default, LLM config follows the same-source principle; and the suggestion loop has upgraded from “just recorded” to “accept = execute” — automation/todo suggestions really become tasks.

ACT 5 · Anti-poisoningAuto-extracted items are pending; confirm before recall
